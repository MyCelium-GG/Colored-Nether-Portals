# Colored Nether Portals

Any color for your Nether portals. Sneak and right-click a portal with an
empty hand to open the color menu, or simply right-click it with a dye.

- **Styles:** solid, gradient (2 to 16 colors, fading smoothly bottom to top),
  rainbow, color cycle and pulse, with three speeds
- **Colors:** the 16 dyes, 8 extra colors, a color picker and hex codes
- **Everything matches:** the portal's particles, and the swirl over your
  screen while you travel, take its color
- **Multiplayer:** colors are saved with the world and every player with the
  mod sees them; vanilla players see the usual purple
- **Any server:** on servers without the mod your colors stay on your computer
- **Config:** `allowAnimated`, `dyeColoring`, `consumeDye`, editable live with `/mycel set`

Requires **Mycel** (install it alongside). Minecraft 26.3, 26.2 and 26.1 on Fabric, Forge
and NeoForge, Java 25. Docs: https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/colorednetherportals/
