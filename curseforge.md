# Colored Nether Portals

Any color for your Nether portals. Sneak and right-click a portal with an empty hand to open the color menu, or simply right-click it with a dye.

![Two Nether portals colored with gradients, with particles in matching colors](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/colorednetherportals/img/gradients.png)

- Styles: solid, gradient (2 to 16 colors, fading bottom to top), rainbow, color cycle and pulse, with three speeds
- Colors: the 16 dyes, 8 extra colors, a color picker and hex codes
- The particles and the swirl over your screen while you travel take the portal's color
- Colors are saved with the world and every player with the mod sees them

## Install

Install it on both sides to share colors. Vanilla players can join and see the usual purple. On a server without the mod, your colors stay on your computer.

Requires [Mycel](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mycel): install it alongside this mod. Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge, Java 25. On Fabric you also need Fabric API.

Every setting and command is on the [wiki](https://wiki.mycelium-gg.org/colorednetherportals/).
