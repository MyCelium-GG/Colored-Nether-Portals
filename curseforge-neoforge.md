# Colored Nether Portals (NeoForge)

Any color for your Nether portals: sneak and right-click a portal for a
color menu, or right-click it with a dye. Solid colors, smooth gradients,
rainbows, color cycles and pulses, with colored particles and travel swirl.

- Dye colors, a color picker and hex codes; gradients through up to 16 colors
- Saved with the world and shown to every player with the mod
- Works on servers without the mod too (only you see the colors)

Requires **Mycel**. Needs NeoForge 26.3.0.1-beta+, Minecraft 26.3, Java 25.
